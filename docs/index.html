The work comes first · Product vision

MOK → PLAYER → PLAY → ROK → REVIEW

업무의 몫을 정하고, 적합한 선수를 선택하고, 실행의 기록으로 다음 판을 개선합니다.

01 · MOK

Define the work

Set the outcome, scope, authority and evidence required before choosing a Player.

02 · PLAYER

Choose who can do it

Consider capability, authority, privacy, cost and capacity. Model choice is one part of Player selection.

03 · PLAY

Execute within authority

Use a Baton to connect a human decision, a bounded action and its evidence. Challenge a result when the evidence falls short.

04 · ROK

Keep the work record

Connect the MOK, selected Player, evidence, revisions and final acceptance into a record of what happened.

05 · REVIEW

Improve the next Play

Review outcomes to design a better MOK, select a better-suited Player and refine the authority boundary.

This cycle describes the product direction. Automated Player routing, Challenge workflows, ROK analytics and learning from Review are not delivered by the current demo.

Player Pool · Product vision

Human + AI + Service

The MOK defines what is needed. The Player Pool gives you choices.

Human

A Captain, teammate or specialist can contribute judgment, execution and review.

AI

Claude, Codex, Gemini, sovereign AI and specialist AI can be candidates for different work.

Service

An approved tool or service can perform a bounded task and return evidence.

Provider names illustrate the vision, not shipped integrations or partnerships. Available quota is a capacity signal; it does not grant authority.
